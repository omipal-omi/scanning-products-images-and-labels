Packaged Commodities Rules, 2026 — Statutory Guide

Every pre-packaged commodity in India is governed by the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2026 under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009. The principal display panel of every package must contain the following declarations:

1. Maximum Retail Price — Rule 6(1)(e):
Must state MRP inclusive of all taxes. Format: MRP ₹ XX.XX (incl. of all taxes). Dual pricing or altered MRP is punishable.
2. Net Quantity — Rule 6(1)(d):
Must be declared in standard metric units: Mass in grams (g) / kilograms (kg), Volume in milliliters (ml) / liters (l), or piece count (N / pieces). Non-standard units are non-compliant.
3. Country of Origin — Rule 6(1)(f):
Must clearly state "Country of Origin: [Country]" or "Made in [Country]". For imported goods, the importer's name and address must be stated.
4. Date of Manufacture / Packing — Rule 6(1)(c):
Must declare Month and Year of manufacture or pre-packaging (e.g. Mfd: 08/2026, Pkd: Aug 2026).
5. Consumer Care Details — Rule 6(1)(g):
Must provide contact details of person/office for consumer grievances, including official telephone/toll-free number and email ID.
Rule 7 Font Size Mandates: Minimum numeral font height depends on net quantity:
  • Up to 50g/ml: Minimum 1.0 mm height
  • 50g to 200g/ml: Minimum 1.5 mm height
  • 200g to 1kg/l: Minimum 2.0 mm height
  • Above 1kg/l: Minimum 4.0 mm height
